Six new research and portfolio workflows, built directly into your strategy notes. Existing strategies give the same backtests as in 0.13.3.

- **Drag any number in a rule.** Drag any number in a strategy block (or hold Option and drag for finer steps) to see the equity curve, metrics and delta against the saved note update live without declaring parameters first. Release to keep the value, or snap back.
- **Per-clause attribution and muting.** Prefix any rule line with `~` (or click a clause in the collapsible Clauses section) to mute it and see the exact change in Sharpe, CAGR, max drawdown and trade count. Each clause is tagged as `key`, `no effect`, or `hurts`, and muted lines can be removed in one click.
- **Automatic stress checks.** Every run includes a 2x/5x cost multiplier, 1-bar entry delay, best-1% day removal and parameter sensitivity check right below the result, without opening a separate dialog.
- **Bar probing and trade-cluster brushing.** Click any bar on the price chart to see every condition's value on that date, which condition blocked an entry or exit, and what threshold change would have flipped it. Drag across losing trades on the equity curve to rank the indicators that best separate them from the rest, with permutation p-values and one-click filter insertion.
- **Composable strategy graphs.** Share definitions across notes with `signal`, `regime` and `universe` blocks, inherit from a parent strategy with `extends: [[Parent]]` and `base`, and see an automatic diff card comparing the child to its parent. Edits to an upstream block refresh downstream strategies and roll up trial counts across the family for "Was it luck?".
- **Active book evaluation (`+ In My Book`).** Mark an `account` block with `book: active` to evaluate any strategy against your current portfolio—showing marginal Sharpe, max drawdown, tail-drawdown correlation, sleeve overlap and net transition orders with a weight slider.
- **Live tracking, fill ledger and stop rules.** Deploy a strategy straight from its card menu (`live since:`), append confirmed fills and broker CSV imports to a deduplicated vault fill ledger, compare modeled vs. realized equity on one chart, and declare `invalidate when` rules that warn as live metrics approach your stop threshold and pause trade signals if tripped.
- **Command line and MCP server.** `check` now resolves `extends:` and `[[...#...]]` links across the vault and validates `signal`, `regime`, `universe` and `invalidate when` blocks.
