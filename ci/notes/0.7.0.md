A new way to read a result. The figures are the same as in 0.6.0; what changed is how they are drawn.

- **A verdict first.** A sentence sets return per year and worst fall against buy and hold ("7.2% a year against 10.6% for buy and hold — but its worst fall was 24%, not 55%."), and a second one compares the Sharpe ratios, the time in the market and the years it was ahead. The wording is fixed and filled in with the run's figures.
- **Figures side by side.** Each figure is a row with the strategy's value, the benchmark's, a dot for each and the difference. "More figures" adds the Sortino ratio, best and worst year and month, months with a gain and the longest time below a high.
- **A chart you can read.** The gap between the two lines is shaded in the colour of whichever is ahead, the market's big falls are shaded and named, and a strip shows the time held (hatched where short). Move across it and the line above reads that bar: date, values, how far below the high, what was held and which fall it lies in. All, 10Y, 5Y and 1Y show a shorter span.
- **At the next close.** What it holds now, the next order in a sentence, and a scale of the closes that would give it, with the last close marked. A mark by the title says when an order is within 2% ("0.33% from a sell").
- **Sections as tabs.** Trades, Years, Months and Regimes and crises open one at a time, each with a line of what is in it: every trade's return as a bar (click one to find it in the list), the gap to buy and hold each year, a calendar of monthly returns, and the market falls as rows on one scale.
- **Shorter block text and folded line.** The block's settings fold away behind "Show the whole block", since the line under the title says them in words. A folded result shows a small chart of its value.
- Tried in a running Obsidian, light and dark, at desktop, preview-panel and phone width, with long, short, leveraged, ranked, weekly and hourly blocks.
