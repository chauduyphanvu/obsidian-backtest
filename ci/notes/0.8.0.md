More to say in a rule. Every block that ran in 0.7.0 gives the same result in 0.8.0.

- **Definitions.** `uptrend = close > sma(200)` names a condition or a number. Use the name in rules, in a ranking score and in other definitions, as in `buy when uptrend and rsi(2) < 10`; `uptrend of SPY` and `uptrend[1]` read it for another ticker or a bar back. The rule table opens a definition into its parts, and hovering over its name shows what it stands for.
- **Conditions that last.** `close > sma(50) for 3 bars` holds on each of the last 3 bars, `rsi(2) < 10 within 5 bars` on any of them, and `count(close > close[1], 10)` counts the bars it held. `rsi(2) between 10 and 30` sets a range, and `crosses` alone means either way.
- **Rules for a few tickers at once.** `for SPY, QQQ:` gives the rules and stops under it to each ticker listed.
- **Exits that follow the position.** `highest since entry`, `lowest since entry` and `position return` make a chandelier exit or a profit target, as in `sell when close < highest since entry - 3 * atr(22)`.
- **New functions.** `sum`, `dema`, `tema`, `kama`, `aroon_up`, `aroon_down`, `psar`, `cmf`, `linreg`, `rsquared`, `efficiency` and `streak`, with `sqrt`, `log`, `exp` and `if(condition, a, b)` for scores such as Clenow's momentum.
- **Calendar.** `trading_day` and `trading_days_left` count the trading days of the month, for turn-of-the-month rules, and `quarter`, `week_start`, `week_end`, `quarter_start`, `quarter_end`, `year_start` and `year_end` mark the edges of each period.
- A block that already used one of the new words as a parameter name, such as `count = 20`, keeps working: the name means its parameter there.
