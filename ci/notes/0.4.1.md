Fixes from a full trial of the plugin in a running Obsidian. Results of some strategies change:

- **Yahoo dates in summer.** Bars Yahoo stamps at the exchange's midnight, which are every weekly bar and the daily bars of currency pairs, were dated a day early while that exchange was on summer time (SPY's weekly bars fell on Sundays). Each bar is now dated in the exchange's own time zone. Strategies on weekly Yahoo bars, or reading a currency pair with `of`, may give different trades.
- **CSV files with bars other than daily.** A CSV file's lookback before `from` was worked out from the block's `interval` rather than from the file's own rows, so a weekly file started with its indicators still warming up, and a minute file was loaded whole and could be refused by the size limit. It is now counted in the file's own rows. Daily files give the same results as before.
- **Ticker suggestions** now show while you type at a normal pace; before, they appeared only after a pause on one letter.
- **Hovering a chart** no longer raises an error in Obsidian, and Obsidian no longer puts a tooltip over the Parameters bar and other labelled regions.
- **Completion** goes on offering two-word values after their space: `bars h` offers `bars held`.
- **Messages**: a CSV file that can't be read is named, a period out of range says the range (1 to 10000), and a cash-rate ticker that can't load says it comes from the block's `cash rate` line or the "Interest on cash" setting.
