More changes from the automated review of the Community plugins directory. The backtests and their figures are the same as in 0.13.1.

- **Settings search.** In Obsidian 1.13 and later, the search of the settings window finds the settings of the plugin. Older versions of Obsidian show the same settings tab as before. The note on what the plugin sends to the strategy writer is now under "Provider".
- **Fewer review warnings.** The review of 0.13.1 found 33 warnings. 22 are fixed. The wavy line under a problem and the dotted line under the name of a figure are now drawn as backgrounds. The folded line of a result places its parts without `display: contents`. The plugin styles use no `!important`. The 11 warnings that remain are about the command line, which does not run in Obsidian.
