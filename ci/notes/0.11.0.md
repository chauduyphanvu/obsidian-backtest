A new design for every surface of the plugin. The figures and the backtests are the same as in 0.10.0.

- **The top of a result.** One line under the title says what the strategy holds and how near its next order is: "Long SPY since 2026-04-09 · +8.6%" and "Sells unless it closes above 730.27 · 0.01% away", in amber within 2% of the last close. Click it for a chart of the last 30 closes against the price of the order. It replaces the chips and the "At the next close" panel. The save buttons, "Start a forward test" and "Save this note's results to its properties" are in a menu (three dots) at the right of the title.
- **Figures.** Each figure has a mark that shows who did better, and the head counts them: "Better on 3 · worse on 2". Move the pointer on a figure to see where it comes from on the chart.
- **Chart.** The read-out keeps its place, the spans (All, 10Y, 5Y, 1Y) move the chart to the new span, and the largest gap between the lines is named ("Buy & hold ahead").
- **Tuning.** Drag a number in the rules to change a parameter, or use its slider. While you drag, the run as written stays on the chart as a dashed line, each figure says how far it moved, and the tabs wait until you let go. After "Try other values", the sliders show the return per year of each value on their tracks.
- **Tabs and tools.** Trades, Years, Months and Regimes are tabs with a line of what is in them. The other sections are round buttons under "Test the idea".
- **Folded results** are on fixed columns, with a small chart, so a stack of folded blocks reads as one list.
- **Problems.** A block that cannot run shows its own lines, the problem under the line that has it, and a button that writes the correction into the note ("Change to sma"). The orders window and the `backtests` table have the same button.
- **Today's orders.** A row for each order, close to watch and problem, with a tag of what to do. A close to watch has a bar that fills as the close comes nearer. Holdings are folded.
- **`backtests` table.** Six columns with a small chart, rows that move to their place when they finish or when you sort, and a narrow layout.
- **Editor.** Over an indicator in a rule, the help also shows its value at the last close on the block's ticker, from the saved prices.
- **Motion.** A result arrives once, the first time it shows in a session, and folding, tabs and panels ease open and closed. With "Reduce motion" on in your system's settings, every change happens at once.
- **Contrast.** All text has a contrast of 4.5:1 or more with its background, in Obsidian's default light and dark themes. The rules keep your theme's code colours, mixed with the text colour where necessary.
